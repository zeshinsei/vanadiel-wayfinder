# Quickstart

1. Buy the game: [Square Enix shop (NA)](https://na.store.square-enix-games.com/final-fantasy_-xi_-ultimate-collection-seekers-edition---digital) or [Square Enix shop (EU)](https://eu.store.square-enix-games.com/final-fantasy_-xi_-ultimate-collection-seekers-edition---digital) or [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/230330/FINAL_FANTASY_XI_Ultimate_Collection_Seekers_Edition/)
   - The Free Trial ([NA](https://na.store.square-enix-games.com/final-fantasy_-xi_-free-trial---digital) or [EU](https://eu.store.square-enix-games.com/final-fantasy_-xi_-free-trial---digital?searchid=0&search_query=final+fantasy+xi+free)) will become less restrictive in the future
3. Download and install the game: [Written guide](https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/Install_Guide)
4. Set up a PlayOnline and SquareEnix account: [Video guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qldwt7F3VAA&)
5. Start PlayOnline and allow it to patch PlayOnline and FFXI
